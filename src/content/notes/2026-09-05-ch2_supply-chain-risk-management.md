**Intermediaries - **distribute, promote, store, or sell goods.

| **Manufacturer** | Produces the goods from raw materials |
| --- | --- |
| **Distributor** | Handles logistics,<font style="color:#DF2A3F;"> regional coverage</font>, and inventory for multiple wholesalers |
| **Wholesaler** | Buys in bulk from distributors, sells in smaller batches to retailers |
| **Retailer** | Sells directly to consumers |
| **Customer** | Final recipient and user of the product |


```plain
Manufacturer → Distributor → Wholesaler → Retailer → Customer
```

Each arrow represents a **transaction between distinct organizations**, and each node is an **organizational role** in the supply chain:

So when you ask “where is the organization?”— the answer is: **everywhere**. The supply chain is a network of organizations, each performing a specialized function.

A **distributor** acts as an **exclusive or authorized intermediary for a manufacturer**, promoting and selling products to downstream businesses like wholesalers or retailers, often **taking ownership** of the goods. They handle regional or national delivery  --  区域物流、库存管理和市场覆盖  

A **wholesaler** buys in bulk **from distributors** or **directly from manufacturers** and sells to **retailers**, who then sell to consumers. 

A **retailer** buys products from wholesalers or distributors and sells them directly to the end **consumers**, typically in smaller quantities and at a higher markup.

1. **Vertical integration 垂直整合**
    - in house
    - Relationship with suppliers - Distant
    - 专有或定制化生产 Specialised or proprietary production
    - High control, low <u>flexibility</u>
        * **flexibility as a response to uncertainty and change**.  
2. **Vertical disintegration 垂直解构（分散化)**
    - Move to **outsourcing**（**外包**生产）
    - Suppliers network
    - 降低成本，专注核心功能 - R&D high cost↑
    - Control ↓, efficiency ↑
3. **Virtual integration 虚拟整合**
    - 完全依赖外部合作伙伴
    - 与供应商建立紧密关系
    - 快速进入市场
    - High flexibility, High dependency 

| Strategy 类型 | Risk Response 应对风险 | Example 举例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vertical Integration**<br/> 垂直整合 | ❌ Slow to adapt<br/>     应变慢，风险集中 | **Traditional auto manufacturers**<br/>传统汽车制造商 |
| **Vertical Disintegration** 垂直解构 | ⚠️ Can shift suppliers<br/>可更换供应商，降低部分风险 | Apparel brands **outsourcing** to Asia<br/>服装品牌外包至亚洲 |
| **Virtual Integration**<br/>虚拟整合 | ✅ Rapid adaptation<br/>快速应变，风险分散 | E-commerce platforms with API-linked suppliers<br/>通过API连接供应商的电商平台 |

### 二、SCM RISK
###### What motivated RISK management in supply chain？
+ **Global competition** - Supply chains stretch across borders - involving mutiple vendors, regulations and geopolitical risks.
    - A disruptin in one country can ripple acrooss the entire chain.
+ **Technological change **
    - Cybersecurity
    - The usage of advanced technology leads to difference
+ **Continual search for competitve advantage**
    - Businesses constantly seek ways to differentiate—faster delivery, lower costs, better quality.
    - Pushing for leaner operations（浪费↓） or just-in-time inventory can increase vulnerability to disruptions.
        * JIT - 保持最低库存，降低成本。

+ **<font style="background-color:#FBDE28;">Risk mitigation 如何缓解风险？</font>**
+ 风险缓解发生在实际风险事件发生之前因为它涉及采取主动措施来降低潜在威胁的可能性或影响
1. 管理**不确定性 uncertainty** - 供给 supply、需求 demand、产品 product management、信息 information management.
2.  **Network design** can enable better control of supply risks  
    - 供应链的结构设计（比如是否有多个供应商、是否有区域分布）会影响风险控制能力  
3.  Strategies such as **product pricing** can control demand to a degree  
    - 通过定价策略控制需求 -  通过调整价格，可以在供应紧张时抑制需求，或在库存过剩时刺激购买。  
4.  System can provide greater information visibility across supply chain members  -  信息共享是降低风险的关键。越多成员能实时看到数据，越能快速响应。

### SCM Risk Strategy
###### 1. Flexible Supply Base
Mitigates risk from sole sourcing through **multiple suppliers**. 

通过多个供应商来降低“单一来源”的风险。 

###### 2. Make and Buy Strategy
Including **external** production as an** alternative source.** 

结合自制与外购，确保在内部产能不足时有外部支持。

###### 3. Economic Supply Incentives
Used even if production cannot be shifted. 

即使无法转移生产，也可通过经济激励保障供应

###### 4. Flexible Transportation
Provides assurance of delivery. 

使用多种运输方式或备用物流渠道，确保交付可靠性。 

###### 5. Strategic Stock
Safety stock without excessive inventory cost. 

设置关键物料的战略库存，避免全面囤货带来的高成本。

###### 6. Dynamic Pricing and Promotions
Forms of revenue management. 

通过动态定价和促销策略来调节需求，缓解供应压力。 

###### 7. Postponement
Improves the ability to manages supplies and accomplishes greater product flexibility;

Delays product differentiation until closer to the time of sale. 

延迟产品定制或差异化，直到更接近销售时点，以应对需求变化。 

像服装品牌先生产白色T恤，等订单确定后再染色。

######  8. Dynamic Assortment Planning   动态商品组合规划  
based on influencing consumer product demand by display location;

 通过调整商品在货架或页面上的展示位置，影响消费者的购买偏好和需求分布。  

 电商平台首页推荐、实体店货架布局优化。  

######  9. Silent Product Rollover  静默产品替换  
involves slow leading of new products without formal announcement.

 不进行正式宣传的情况下，逐步引入新产品，减少库存冲击和市场不确定性。
