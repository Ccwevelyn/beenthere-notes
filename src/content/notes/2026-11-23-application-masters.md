申请页上会看到 MS、MEng、MPhil、授课硕这些名字。MS 和 MEng 主要出现在美国院校；香港、新加坡、英国常用的分法不一样。下面按地区把常见项目说清楚。我不会按地区替你做推荐，选哪一种取决于你自己要什么。

## 香港与新加坡

这两地大体接近，硕士常见分成两类。

**授课型硕士（taught）**  
以上课、作业、考试或 capstone 为主。全日制多为一年，也有一年半；学制有时可以延长。如果读 part-time，时间往往接近全日制的两倍。

**研究型硕士（research），一般叫 MPhil**  
以研究为主，后面常常接博士。适合已经有研究经历、明确想读博的人。funding 情况并不统一：以前多数带 funding，近几年经费紧，自费的 MPhil 也会出现。申请前要看清楚这一届有没有 funding、要不要自己交学费。

## 英国

英国授课型硕士偏多。全日制常见九个月左右（有的写成一年，实际上课加论文大概是这个长度）。研究型也有，但对大多数申请授课项目的人来说，先按「九个月、以课为主」来理解即可。

## 美国：MS 和 MEng

MS、MEng 这类写法，主要是美国院校在用。

**MEng**  
更接近授课、职业导向，学制常见一年半到两年。

**MS**  
学制大体按两年来准备。它不一定等于「纯科研」：有的 MS 偏研究，有的偏职业，有的进校后再选 track。

MS 内部还要再看一层：

- **申请时就分开**：网申页面上已经拆成不同 track，录取难度可能不一样，交表前就要选定。
- **进校后再选**：录取门槛同一条，进去之后再选 capstone、thesis 等方向。

所以不要只看三个字母。同一所学校的 MS，有的进门前就定方向，有的进门后再分；难度是不是同一档，也要看申请页怎么写。若目标是就业，MEng 或授课硕通常更合适；若目标是读博，选一个进校前就分开、偏研究的 MS track，往往更稳。

注：也有人先读授课硕或 capstone，把两年的课尽量压进一年，剩下的时间去联系老师、进实验室攒研究经历。人生没有固定答案，但前提是课真能提前修完，实验室也接得住。

## 怎么对应到自己

- 想上课、一年左右结束、先就业：香港 / 新加坡的授课硕，或英国的授课硕，或美国的 MEng，更接近。
- 想做研究、以后读博：先看 MPhil（港新）以及美国里明确 thesis / research track 的 MS。
- 时间能不能拉长、能不能读 part-time：港新授课硕往往可以谈延长；part-time 大约翻倍。
- 美国项目务必打开申请页：MS 和 MEng 的导向、track 是申请时定还是入学后选，各校不同。

没有哪一个地区「更好」。先写清自己要上课还是做研究、能读多久、要不要 funding，再去对号。

<div class="i18n-en">

Application pages use names such as MS, MEng, MPhil, and taught master's. MS and MEng are mainly a US habit. Hong Kong, Singapore, and the UK sort programmes differently. Below I describe the usual types by region. I am not ranking regions. Which one you need depends on what you want.

## Hong Kong and Singapore

These two are close enough to treat together. Master's degrees usually split into two kinds.

**Taught master's**  
Coursework, assignments, exams, or a capstone. Full-time is often one year, sometimes one and a half; the period can sometimes be extended. Part-time is often about twice the full-time length.

**Research master's, usually an MPhil**  
Research first, often as a step toward a PhD. It suits people who already have research experience and want a doctorate. Funding is mixed. Many used to come with funding; budgets have been tighter in recent years, so self-funded MPhils show up too. Check whether this intake has funding and whether you pay tuition.

## The United Kingdom

Taught master's are the majority. Full-time is often about nine months (some pages say one year; taught term plus dissertation is roughly that). Research degrees exist, but if you are applying for a taught programme, start from "about nine months, mainly coursework."

## The United States: MS and MEng

Labels such as MS and MEng are mainly used in the US.

**MEng**  
Closer to a taught, professionally oriented degree. Length is often one and a half to two years.

**MS**  
Plan for about two years. It is not automatically "research only": some MS degrees lean research, some lean professional, and some let you pick a track after you arrive.

Look one layer deeper inside an MS:

- **Split at application**: the portal already lists separate tracks. Admission difficulty may differ. You choose before you submit.
- **Split after arrival**: one admissions bar; once in, you choose capstone, thesis, and similar options.

Do not stop at the three letters. In one school an MS may lock the track before you apply; in another you choose later. Whether difficulty is the same also depends on how the application page is written. If the aim is a job, an MEng or a taught master's is usually the closer fit. If the aim is a PhD, an MS whose research track is already split at application is usually the more stable choice.

Note: some people take a taught master's or a capstone track, finish two years of coursework in one year if the rules allow, and spend the remaining time with a supervisor and in a lab. There is no single answer, but only if you can really finish the courses early and a lab will take you.

## Matching this to yourself

- Coursework, about a year, then work: a taught master's in Hong Kong or Singapore, a UK taught master's, or a US MEng is closer.
- Research, then a PhD: start with an MPhil in Hong Kong or Singapore, and with US MS programmes that clearly offer a thesis or research track.
- Extra time or part-time: taught programmes in Hong Kong and Singapore often allow an extension; part-time is often double.
- For the US, open the application page: orientation of MS vs MEng, and whether the track is chosen at apply time or after arrival, varies by school.

No region is "better." Write down whether you want courses or research, how long you can study, and whether you need funding, then match the type.
